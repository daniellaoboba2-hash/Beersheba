About Beersheba

A home for stories worth getting lost in.
Beersheba is a curated fiction platform built around cinematic, binge-worthy stories — romance, fantasy, and drama told in serialized chapters. Every story on the platform is published and managed by the Beersheba team. We built Beersheba around a simple idea: reading should feel immersive from the first page. That means thoughtful cover art, a distraction-free reader, and a library that's genuinely yours once you unlock a story — no subscriptions required for standard access, and a straightforward Lifetime VIP option for readers who want everything. Beersheba is currently in active development. Features like translation and audiobook narration are being built out — you'll see clear "prototype" labeling anywhere a feature isn't fully live yet, because we'd rather be upfront about what's real than pretend something works when it doesn't.

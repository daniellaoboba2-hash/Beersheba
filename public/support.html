Support

Answers to common questions, or reach us directly below.

Account

How do I create an account?

Tap Account in the navigation, then Create an account. You'll need a name, email, and password.

I forgot my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. You'll get a reset link (shown directly in this prototype since no email server is connected yet).

Can I change my email or username?

Yes — from Account → Profile. Email changes require your current password and can't collide with another account's email.

Payment

What are my access options?

One-time access ($1 for 20 chapters, $3 for 30, $5 for 50 chapters of one story of your choice), or Lifetime VIP ($30 one-time) for ongoing access to the eligible library.

Can I reuse a one-time purchase on a second book?

No — each one-time purchase unlocks exactly one story. Once you've picked your book, that choice is final.

Are purchases refundable?

See our Refund Policy for details.

Reading & Library

Where do my unlocked stories go?

My Library, split into Continue Reading, Purchased, VIP Collection, Completed, and Bookmarks.

Does my reading progress save?

Yes, automatically, tied to your account — it'll be right where you left off on any device.

Can I change the font, theme, or line spacing?

Yes — Account → Reading Preferences, or the in-reader Text/Theme controls.

VIP

What does Lifetime VIP include?

Access to the eligible library, VIP-exclusive stories, new releases, and PDF downloads where enabled — see the VIP page for full details.

PDF Downloads

Why can't I download a PDF for a book I own?

PDF access depends on the specific book and your access type (VIP vs. standard purchase) — not every story has PDF downloads enabled.

Technical

Voice Reader isn't working.

Voice Reader uses your browser's built-in text-to-speech. Not all browsers support this — Chrome, Edge, and Safari generally do.

Translation isn't producing results.

Translation is prototype-labeled until a live provider is connected — you'll see a clear message rather than an incorrect translation.

Still need help?

Email us directly at support@beersheba.app, or use the contact form.
